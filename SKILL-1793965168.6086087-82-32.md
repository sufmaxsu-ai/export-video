# Hand-drawn pencil film (Canvas 2D → Chromium → FFmpeg)

## 0. What to reuse from this project
Copy `public/web/js/{engine,characters,set,timeline,film}.js`, `public/web/preview.html`, and
`scripts/{render-frames.mjs,export-video.sh,make-audio.py,qa.py}`. Replace the cast/set/timeline; keep the engine,
the contract and the tools. Everything is plain JS (classic `<script>` tags), so it runs from `file://`, from any static
server, inside an `<iframe>`, and in Playwright.

## 2. The drawing engine — the things that made it look hand-made
1. **Unit = union of primitives with ONE outline.** Stroke every primitive thick (2×w) on a scratch canvas, then
   `destination-out` the exact fills of all primitives → only the outer half of the union contour remains.
   Arm = shoulder disc + upper-arm capsule + forearm capsule in one unit → no line at the elbow, no gap.
   Each primitive is filled separately (`fill()` per polygon) to avoid non-zero winding holes.
2. **Opaque exact under-layer, misregistered colour on top.** Fill the exact shape with a light tint (hides what is
   behind, e.g. the skull under the hair), then the full colour shifted ±4 px with hatching + paper tooth knocked out.
   Result: colour spills a little on one side and leaves a pale rim on the other, like real colouring.
3. **Wobble along the normal** with two random sinusoids per contour + a tiny global shift; amplitude ≈1.9 px.
   Seed = `(drawingIndex, semanticId)`; at 3 fps every frame IS a new drawing, so every frame boils.
4. **Line hierarchy:** outline ≈5.2 px × unit multiplier (set pieces 0.55–0.75, characters 1, scaled down with
   distance `k = clamp(s/4.6, .32, 1.12)`); interior lines (folds, lids, fingers) 1.3–2.5 px with taper.
5. **Paper:** one seeded paper canvas (registered, not moving) + a multiply grain pass at the end + warm vignette.
6. **Intro "drawing itself":** frame 0 = blue construction pencil only (`mode.construct`), frame 1 = graphite with
   colour 45 % (jagged colouring edge), frame 2 = finished. Cheap and very effective.

## 3. Characters: rig to place, drawing to finish
- Local units are centimetres, feet origin, facing +x, `dir=-1` mirrors. Proportions in `production/BIBLE.md`.
- 2-bone IK with **imposed bend side** (elbows down/back = +1, knees forward = −1). Seated kids use FK leg angles
  (thigh ~1.4 rad, shin swing ±0.4 alternating) → dangling, swinging feet.
- **Reach assist (important):** a hand target out of reach makes the hand float away from the object.
  `autoReach()` searches the smallest lean/hip change that reaches it, **bounded** (adult lean ≤ 1.12 rad, child ≤ 0.7,
  child slides forward ≤ 12 cm). Every residual miss > 3 cm is logged to `FILM.warnings` and exported in `meta.json`.
  First pass had 41 misses up to 37 cm; unbounded solver produced absurd poses (granny horizontal, kid lying on the
  table). Fix the *staging* (move the cake, let the kid kneel on the chair) rather than stretch the arm.
- Held props are drawn **at the actual wrist position returned by IK** through hooks (`hold.FUnder`, `hold.front`,
  `hold.top`, `hold.BUnder`) placed in the right layer: far hand → body → head → prop → near hand.
- Layer order inside a character: far arm, far leg, near leg, skirt/shorts, torso, apron, neck, (bun), face, hair,
  ear, glasses/eyes/mouth, prop, near arm. Hair after face + opaque under-layer = no residual skull line.
- Walks: compute footsteps in WORLD coordinates, alternate contact/passing drawings (3 fps → ~0.67 s per step),
  planted foot keeps the same world point across frames, heel-strike rot −0.18, push-off +0.35, pelvis lower at contact.

## 4. 3D for depth without breaking the drawing
`F.Cam({x,y,z,yaw,f,vy})` projects world cm to screen with near-plane clipping. Boxes draw only faces whose normal
faces the camera (each face its own unit → visible edges). Characters are billboards placed by their feet
(scale = f/depth). A hinged oven door is a real rotation about its bottom edge. Yaw 90° gives a frontal insert of a
side-wall object. Actors are depth-sorted by their Z. Keep chairs/table/props in the same depth-sorted layer for
every shot that sees them, otherwise objects "appear by magic".

## 5. Composition rules that mattered (vertical 9:16)
- First pass put everything in the upper half (horizon too high, camera too far): characters must be ~35–50 % of the
  frame height; lower the camera eye (110–130 cm) and push `vy` down; fill the bottom with a rug/cat/foreground.
- Keep faces between y≈450 and y≈1450; nothing important in the top 150 / bottom 250 px.
- Add cheap set life: ceiling beams, hanging herbs, clock whose hand jumps after the baking cut-away, hourglass sand,
  empty swing still swinging after the girl jumped off, ball rolling to rest, finger dent that stays in the cream.

## 6. Pipeline and delivery
```bash
./scripts/export-video.sh web/preview.html final/film.mp4            # HTML/JS -> Chromium frames -> FFmpeg (+auto foley)
./scripts/export-video.sh production/frames final/film.mp4 --fps 3   # image sequence
./scripts/export-video.sh final/film.mp4 final/film.webm             # convert (VP9/Opus), .mov too
```
MP4 settings: `-c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p -r 3 -c:a aac -ar 48000 -ac 2 -movflags +faststart`.
Preview: cache each finished drawing in an offscreen canvas, pre-draw in idle time, play with `requestAnimationFrame`
and `frame = floor(t*fps)`; seek bar = frame index. The web folder must contain the MP4, the JS, the tools and use
relative paths only; test it from a copy in /tmp with `python3 -m http.server`.

## 7. QA loop (do it after every change)
1. `qa.py sheet` (10 columns) → composition, continuity, missing objects.
2. 4–8 frame strips at 400–480 px → faces, hands, props, overlaps. Zoom crops for faces.
3. Read `meta.json.warnings` (IK misses).  4. `qa.py onion` on walks/runs.  5. `qa.py stats` (blank/duplicate/flicker).
6. Byte-compare a frame rendered twice.  7. `qa.py probe` on the MP4 (codec, pix_fmt, 3/1 fps, 60 frames, 20 s, AAC, faststart).
Typical defects found here: kid ear read as a spectacle lens (round ear too close to the eyes → C-shaped ear further back,
hidden under hair for the girl); front pigtail covering the face; bowl lifted to face height while pouring; dent drawn on
the wrong side; speech-bubble text overflowing; sky visible under a ground plane that starts too far.

## 8. Pitfalls
- Never `Math.random()` while drawing; never seed with wall-clock time.
- `ffmpeg -encoders | grep -q` under `set -o pipefail` gives false negatives (SIGPIPE) → store the list first.
- Convert RGBA→RGB before saving JPEG; PIL images from canvas PNGs are RGBA.
- A texture never rescues a weak pose: fix staging and silhouettes first, then marks.
- 3 fps is deliberate: each of the 60 drawings must read alone as an illustration.
