# Hand-drawn pencil film in JavaScript (proven on « Mamie fait un gâteau », 60 drawings, 3 fps, 1080×1920)

Read together with `ANIMATION_ENVIRONMENT_GUIDE.md` (tools, commands, errors) and `SKILLS_USAGE_NOTES.md` (analysis of the 11 source repos).

## 1. Architecture that worked (copy it)
```
public/web/js/pencil.js      seeded RNG (explicit state, snapshot/restore), Catmull-Rom, pencil(), colour(), shape(), poly(), tube(), paper grain
public/web/js/characters.js  figures in local cm (y down, floor y=0, facing +x), IK, arms/legs as tubes, heads, expressions
public/web/js/world.js       pinhole camera proj(X,Y,Z), garden, wall with real holes, 3D props, cake states
public/web/js/timeline.js    BEATS, cumulative pose keys per character, stateAt(f) for props, CUES for sound
public/web/js/film.js        drawFrame(ctx,f): seed=f, depth-sorted drawables, held props, marks; window.FILM + window.__anim
public/web/js/player.js      requestAnimationFrame clock → frame=floor(t*fps); redraw only on frame change
public/web/scripts/          render-frames.mjs (Playwright), make-audio.mjs (pure Node WAV synth), export-video.sh (HTML | image seq | video)
```
One function draws a frame; preview, React page and exporter all call it. Export = `?export=1` bare 1080×1920 canvas → `__anim.drawFrame(i)` → `toDataURL` → PNG → ffmpeg.

## 2. Drawing recipe (what made it look hand-drawn)
- **Boil on purpose**: `seedFrame(f)` at the start of every frame; every contour/fill/hatch uses that stream → every sheet differs, yet the same f always gives the same pixels (verified by SHA-1 re-render). Do NOT put `Math.random()` or time in drawing.
- **Pencil line** = sampled spline, 2 passes, per-segment random width/alpha, low-frequency wobble along the normal, small gaps, overshoot on closed shapes. Base width ≈ 4.2 px at 1080 wide (user wanted visible, slightly big lines). Scale the pencil down for distant figures (`G.LWS = clamp(0.3+0.14k, .5, 1)`).
- **Colouring in 3 layers**: (1) opaque base = colour mixed 40 % with paper, exactly inside the contour → **hides every line behind the shape** (this is how you remove residual lines: skull under hair, limb joins); (2) full colour shifted ±4–5 px (spills and gaps like a child/illustrator); (3) crayon hatching clipped to a *shifted* copy of the shape. Then multiply a paper-grain canvas + warm vignette over the frame.
- **No seams in limbs**: build each limb as ONE tube polygon around a joint chain (shoulder→elbow→wrist→knuckles), outline everything except the start cap (`capStart:false`) so it grows out of the torso/sleeve. Draw skin forearm+hand first, then the sleeve tube over its start. Thumb = tiny tube with open start.
- **Head**: skull + nose + chin in ONE closed contour, ear, then hair mass drawn after (covers the top of the skull). Eyes as dots with highlight / ∩ arcs (happy) / lines; mouths as small filled shapes. Blush = short pink crayon strokes.
- **Characters need a neck**: kids' shoulders at chin level made the sleeve cross the face. Keep chin → shoulder ≥ 8 cm and hide the neck tube's ends under collar and head.

## 3. Depth / "3D" that stays natural
- Pinhole camera: `x = 540 + (X-camX)·F/Z`, `y = HZ + (CAMH-Y)·F/Z`. A lateral camera move gives true parallax for free. Put the floor/wall junction ~60 % down.
- Wall drawn with even-odd holes → garden (sky, trees, swing pendulum, fence, washing line) is only visible through window/door; kids beyond the wall are drawn inside that clip, inside the room they are depth-sorted.
- 3D props as projected quads: counter (top seen from above, side face appears during the pan), oven door rotating on its bottom hinge, open glass door leaf, table + gingham cloth, perspective ellipses for plates/rug/cake.
- **Occlusion tricks**: table front cloth drawn AFTER seated kids (knees go under); a character behind a table is drawn before it, then a second "arms only" pass clipped below the table's back edge, restoring the RNG snapshot so the arm is pixel-identical.
- Character size follows Z automatically (figure scale = F/Z). Do not rotate flat drawings in 3D; use redrawn ¾ views.

## 4. Animation method
- Exposure sheet = cumulative keys per character (`[frame, {overrides}]`); non-key frames are numeric breakdowns, but every frame is fully redrawn. Discrete fields (face, expression, held prop) step; `face` must never be interpolated (scale 0!).
- Hands target **world points** `W(X,Y,Z,dx,dy)` resolved through the screen into the character's local cm at draw time → the hand lands on the bowl, oven handle, plate… Held props are drawn from the real hand geometry (compute arm IK before drawing the prop).
- Reach check: arm length vs. target; add lean (torso rotation around the hip) and crouch (knees bend via leg IK) instead of stretching.
- At 3 fps: one step per drawing; alternate contact poses and advance the body by exactly one step length so the planted foot keeps its world position. Runs: alternate A/B with lift; secondary motion (pigtails, skirt sway, swing decay, ball roll).
- Story warmth = many small reads, one at a time: humming notes, peek in the oven, clock racing, "!" on the timer, hip-bump to close the oven, towel over the shoulder, steam fogging the glasses, finger in the cake while she wipes them, innocent whistle, raised eyebrow, legs swinging, cat following the smell, hearts at the end. Every prop has a visible journey (never pops in/out).

## 5. QC loop (do it every iteration)
1. `node public/web/scripts/render-frames.mjs preview.html /tmp/f --frames 0,12,24,36,47,55` (≈0.4 s/frame) → contact sheet with Pillow → look.
2. Full-resolution crops of faces/hands; every held object; every occlusion.
3. Full render + `python3 qc/qc.py` (missing/blank/identical frames, luminance flicker, OpenCV decode, onion skins for walk/run).
4. ffprobe the MP4. Test the live page with Playwright (time advances, pause holds, scrub, video metadata, download HEAD).

## 6. What did NOT work / pitfalls
- p5.brush watercolour (ClaudeAnimationBase): beautiful but slow without GPU; Canvas 2D crayon is enough.
- Holding marks across a held drawing (alesha rule) contradicts a "new sheet every frame" brief — follow the brief.
- `waitUntil:'load'` with a big `<video>` stalls tests → use `domcontentloaded`.
- Relative paths in `node -e require(...)` → use absolute paths.
- Drawing props in a callback before the hand exists → crash → `armGeom` first.
- Too-wide camera makes characters tiny on a phone; zoom (F≈1200) until the lead is ~50 % of the frame height.
- Grain at CRF 18 makes big files (≈2.6 MB/s); acceptable, or raise CRF.

## 7. Delivery checklist
MP4 H.264 yuv420p 3 fps 1080×1920 + AAC 48 kHz stereo, `+faststart`; web folder self-contained with relative paths (index.html, preview.html, js/, scripts/, mp4); `export-video.sh` tested in all three modes; exposure sheet, key poses, frames, QC report; three Markdown files.
